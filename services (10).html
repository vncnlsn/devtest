Seismic Permitting

Seismic permitting has been the core of APS’s work for more than 45 years. We personally contact landowners, explain who we represent and what is being requested, negotiate and document property-specific conditions, then oversee compliance in the field to protect all parties and your reputation.

Land access & permitting

  • 2D, 3D, 4D, microseismic & VSP programs
  • Data centers & large-scale development
  • CCUS & solar energy projects
  • Landowner negotiation & property-specific conditions
  • Municipal, county, state & federal approvals

Project management

  • Permit status tracking & comprehensive reporting
  • Landowner payments, check processing & IRS filings
  • Surface damage coordination & settlement
  • Field oversight & compliance management
02

Right-of-Way Acquisition

We secure linear property rights for pipelines, electric transmission, utilities, telecommunications and roads. A rejected agreement, divided interest or utility conflict can change an alignment mid-acquisition; APS identifies conflicts early and keeps ownership, GIS and route changes synchronized.

Property rights

  • Permanent & temporary easements
  • Temporary & additional workspace
  • Ingress/egress & access agreements
  • Property restrictions & restoration terms

Corridor acquisition

  • Corridor ownership, title & encumbrance review
  • Landowner negotiation & compensation
  • Tract acquisition tracking & reporting
  • Agreement execution & documentation
  • Agency, utility & crossing coordination
03

Title & Leasing

Surface and mineral estates may have separated generations ago, and interests may be split among many heirs. APS traces and reconciles chains of title through public records, courthouses and government archives, and carries that information through mapping, negotiation and the field.

Record procurement

  • County/parish courthouses
  • Federal, state & tribal land systems
  • Recorded instruments & historical archives

Scope

  • Full title search to sovereignty
  • Surface & mineral ownership
  • Probate, heirship & fractional interests
  • Title curative & title opinions
  • Lease research, negotiation & acquisition
04

GIS

APS builds project maps and databases that connect ownership and title to the land itself. Historical legal descriptions rarely align cleanly with current parcels, so we reconcile the differences and keep the data current as alignments change.

Compiled project data

  • Surface & mineral ownership
  • Tracts & legal descriptions
  • Topography & infrastructure
  • Project boundaries & alignments

Scope

  • Comprehensive project mapping
  • Legal description & tract platting
  • Spatial data compilation & reconciliation
  • Project database development & management
  • Maps & exhibits
