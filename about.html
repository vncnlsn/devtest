About

We are APS Land.

In August 2026, we became APS Land Services. The new name reflects work we have perfected for decades while carrying forward the standard we started with: hard work, honesty, and being exceptionally good at what we do.

APS has supported hundreds of energy and infrastructure projects across North America and conducted thousands of successful negotiations involving right-of-way, leasing, permitting and land access. We have proven our capabilities in difficult jurisdictions, challenging access and demanding field conditions across private, state, federal and tribal lands.

We continuously refine how we operate while staying focused on what matters most: getting the details right and delivering on time. We take pride in protecting landowners, field crews, the environment and the projects entrusted to us.

Leadership

Meet the team

Mark Fischbuch

President & Founder

Mark began working in the seismic industry in 1980, gaining experience across field operations. He went on to build his own company, using that background to improve the research, information gathering and land access processes that too often delayed field work.

Decades of ownership research, landowner negotiation and field oversight continue to shape how APS works today.

Zane Nelson

Vice President

Zane joined APS in 2010 after distinguishing himself as a leader in geoscience business operations. As CFO he transformed the company’s financial structure, then took on project leadership and day-to-day operations.

Today he oversees APS operations while working closely with clients and project teams, and continues to drive growth and expand capabilities across the industry.
